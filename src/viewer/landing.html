From listing photos to a model in three steps

No design skills needed. Claude, Anthropic's AI, does the design work, and an engine checks that every brick actually holds.

  1. Share the house

    Upload up to 12 photos of the outside, front first, or enter the address. A floor plan is optional and makes the walls exact.

  2. We design the model

    Claude studies the photos and designs the house brick by brick, then fixes anything the checker flags. It takes about 20 minutes, and you can close the tab.

  3. Build it

    Spin the finished model in 3D, follow the building guide step by step, and order the parts from BrickLink or GoBricks.

What you get

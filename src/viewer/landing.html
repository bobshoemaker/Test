How it works

No design skills needed. If you can take a photo, you can do this.

  1. Share the house

    Upload a few photos of the outside, front first, or just enter the address. A floor plan helps, but isn't needed.

  2. We design it

    We turn your photos into a brick model and show it to you in 3D. If the photos leave something out, you can answer a few quick questions about it first.

  3. Build it

    We pack every piece into a kit and send it to your door. Follow the building guide step by step, just like a LEGO® set.

Questions

The things people ask first.

Is it real LEGO?

The bricks are from GoBricks, a compatible brand made to fit LEGO® bricks. You can mix them with bricks you already have. LEGO is a trademark of the LEGO Group, which doesn't sponsor or endorse Brickhouse.

What photos do I need?

A clear photo of the front, plus the sides and back if you have them: up to twelve. Photos you took yourself are best. If a side isn't shown, you can answer a few quick questions about it, and anything we still have to guess is marked as a guess.

Do I need building experience?

No. The guide goes one layer at a time and shows exactly which pieces go in each step, like any LEGO® set. If you've built one of those, you can build this.

How long does it take?

You'll see your house in 3D before anything is made, and you can close the page while we work on it. Your kit usually arrives in two to three weeks. Building takes an evening or two for the Classic size, a weekend or so for the Grand.

What comes in the kit?

Every piece for your house, packed in bags, plus the baseplate it all stands on. The building guide is online, so you can follow it on your phone, tablet or computer, and spin your house in 3D whenever you like.

Can I make one as a gift?

Of course. Use your own photos of their house, or theirs, pick the size that suits them, and have the kit sent straight to them. It's a lovely one to build together.

The owner's photos

Say which view each shows (it tells the design and the map step where the owner stood), and leave out any that would mislead it. Tap a photo to open it full size.

Add photos

Your own references, up to 4: an aerial or satellite view, a side the owner didn't photograph. They go to the design after the owner's, marked as added by our team. Use images we may use for a product we sell.

The house on the map

The buildings near the address, numbered on a public-domain aerial (north up; the red dot is where the address lookup landed, often a few lots off). Pick the house, and the design maps that building instead of choosing one itself.

Instructions for the design

What the design should know or do that the photos don't settle: "the garage is detached, behind the house", "the upper floor overhangs the garage by about 2 ft", "leave out the neighbour's tree". The design follows these.
